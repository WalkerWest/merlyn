Three engagement modes at L2 and L3

DIY, Done-for-you, or Collaborative

At L2 and L3, three engagement modes are available. The mode depends on whether your team builds it, Regularis builds it, or we build it together — and which L1 track your team has been through. Same capability in all three modes; what changes is who owns the operational muscle long-term.

Mode A · DIY

You build it

Your trained team builds and operates after the matching L1 track. Regularis provides advisory and the platform. Lowest ongoing cost; longest time to first value (training + build).

Requires L1 Data Engineering (for L2) or Agent Construction (for L3)

Mode B · Done-for-you

We build it

Regularis delivers turnkey. Your team consumes outputs and supervises through TeamIQ. Most regional businesses start here — fastest path to value, predictable pricing.

Requires L1 AI Fundamentals (for users) or Business Leaders (for executives)

Mode C · Collaborative

We build it together

Regularis architects + your engineers paired daily. Knowledge transfer scoped into engagement. Customers often start Mode C and transition to Mode A after 12-18 months.

Requires L1 Data Eng or Agent Construction (in parallel with build)

After the build

Then you choose who runs it.

Whichever mode built it, the finished system has to run somewhere. Regularis hosts and maintains it, or your own team does — and the decision is reversible. Everything we build is yours to take: open components, documented handover, and no proprietary runtime you cannot operate without us.

We host and maintain

Recurring. We run the system, patch it, monitor it, and keep it working as your processes change. Your team consumes the output.

You host and maintain

You take delivery and run it on your own infrastructure. We hand over the build, the documentation, and the operating knowledge with it.

Not sure where to start? Most businesses begin at L0 to clear the IT foundation, then add L1 to skill up their team. Most L2 and L3 customers start with Mode B (Done-for-you) and graduate to Mode A or C as their internal capability matures.
