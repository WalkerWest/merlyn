Regularis Desk

Some work still needs
a person.

Regularis Desk is the channel between your business processes and the people who run them. When a workflow reaches a step that software can't finish on its own — an application with no API, a judgment call, an approval — Desk brings that step to the right person and carries the result back.

It runs in the browser — there is nothing to install. Your organization provisions a workspace and issues you an account. It is not a chatbot and not a monitoring tool.

Sign in to Desk Request access

How it works

Desk is one end of a governed workflow. The other end is Ordinarium, the discipline that decides what gets routed, to whom, and what happens to the answer.

A process reaches a human step

A workflow needs something only a person can do — a decision, an approval, or an action in a system with no API.

Desk brings it to the right person

The task appears in the tray or the browser, with the context needed to act on it. No inbox to watch, no queue to remember to check.

The result goes back, on the record

The outcome returns to the process and the workflow continues. Who did what, and when, is recorded as part of the normal run — not as extra instrumentation.

It works in both directions

Desk is a channel, not a mailbox.

The business reaches the operator

A running process hands a step to a named person and waits for the answer. This is most of the traffic: approvals, exceptions, judgment calls, and work inside applications that were never built to be automated.

The operator starts the work

A person can also initiate a process from Desk — kicking off a workflow from the desktop or from the page they are already looking at, without switching to another system to do it.

What you need

  • BrowserChrome, Edge, or Firefox — current release
  • AccountA Regularis workspace, provisioned by your organization
  • NetworkOutbound HTTPS to your organization's Regularis endpoint
  • InstallNone — Desk runs in the browser

Need a workspace?

Desk is provisioned per organization. Tell us about your processes and we'll work out what it needs from your environment.
